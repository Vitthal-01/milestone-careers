Common questions

What age group do you work with?+

Primarily students from Class 9 through the first two years of college — anyone facing a stream, course, or early career decision.

Are sessions online or in person?+

Sessions are conducted online over video call, with flexible scheduling around school hours.

Do parents join the sessions?+

The first and last sessions typically include parents; the ones in between are 1:1 with the student, so they can speak freely.

CFA, FRM, or CS — which credential fits your goal?

Professional certifications are increasingly chosen alongside or instead of an MBA — but each leads to a very different career. We help you pick the right one before you invest a year into it.

  • CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst): for equity research, investment management, and portfolio analysis roles.
  • FRM (Financial Risk Manager): for risk management, treasury, and regulatory roles at banks and NBFCs.
  • CS (Company Secretary): for corporate governance, compliance, and legal-adjacent roles.
  • How each credential's exam levels, timelines, and costs compare — and which recruiters actually value most for your target role.
